# PREREG — non-competitive-team matchups and the closing total, walk-forward

**Written 2026-08-23, BEFORE the walk-forward labels are joined to any line.**
Estimated EXACTLY ONCE.

## 0. Provenance, stated honestly

The hypothesis was GENERATED by a descriptive audit (2026-08-23) using
full-season GMM cluster labels on ALL eight seasons: games with exactly one
non-competitive team ran over the closing total by +0.95 (t=2.81), N-N games
under by -1.9 (t=-1.8, n=251). Those labels were lookahead (full-season
outcomes), and the audit included the holdout seasons. THEREFORE: this test is
confirmatory in CONSTRUCTION (prior-only labels, frozen thresholds) but not on
virgin data — the development result must be discounted for having been
glimpsed through the lookahead version, and the genuinely new information is
(a) whether a bettor-feasible classifier reproduces it and (b) the holdout
seasons under that classifier. Both discounts are recorded now.

## 1. Walk-forward classifier, frozen

Per team, per date, using ONLY games already completed that season:

```
margin_td(team, date) = mean(PF - PA) over the team's prior games this season
                        (shift(1) expanding mean; requires >= 10 prior games)
NC(team, date)        = margin_td <= THRESH
```

Margin needs no league demeaning (league margin is identically zero). THRESH
is frozen as the posterior-0.5 boundary of the two-component GMM refit on
DEVELOPMENT team-seasons only (yr <= 2023), projected onto the margin axis,
computed once before any line is read and recorded in the results section.
No other threshold, floor, or window may be tried.

Games where either team lacks 10 prior games are dropped (early season is out
of scope by construction, as in every walk-forward specification in this
project).

## 2. The single test

```
e_close = total - closing_total
e_close ~ a + b_CN * 1(exactly one NC team) + b_NN * 1(both NC)
```

- PRIMARY: `b_CN` (prediction: > 0, the C-N over-run).
- SECONDARY, reported never promoted: `b_NN` (prediction: < 0, low power),
  and the same regression on the OPENING total.
- Windows: development (<= 2023) and holdout (2024-25 AND 2025-26) separately.
- Inference: within-season permutation of the (C-N, N-N) label pair across
  games, 5,000 reps, on the development window.

## 3. Decision rule, fixed now

PASS requires ALL of:
- development `b_CN` > 0 with permutation p < 0.05;
- holdout `b_CN` > 0 (both holdout seasons pooled; per-season signs reported);
- economic relevance: pooled `b_CN` >= +1.0 point (~2.2pp at the money; the
  -110 vig needs ~1.08 points, so anything under +1.0 is real-but-unbettable).

Anything less is recorded as: the descriptive bias does not survive a
bettor-feasible classifier at bettable size, and the team-class question
closes.

## 4. Prohibited

- tuning THRESH, the 10-game floor, or the class count
- alternative class definitions (pace-based, oeff/deff-based, roster-based)
- subsetting by season part, team, or schedule position after seeing results
- promoting `b_NN` or the opening-line rows
- any second estimation

---

## 5. RESULT — estimated once, 2026-08-23. Verdict: FAIL (question closed)

THRESH frozen at **-5.409** margin points (dev-only GMM posterior-0.5
boundary). 8,236 classified games (both teams >= 10 prior); C-N 2,360, N-N 199.

| window | line | b_CN | t | b_NN | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| development | close | **+0.288** | 0.56 | -2.481 | -1.54 |
| holdout 2024-25 | close | -1.912 | -1.63 | -1.098 | -0.36 |
| holdout 2025-26 | close | +2.919 | +2.34 | -0.634 | -0.20 |
| holdout pooled | close | +0.495 | 0.58 | -0.897 | -0.41 |

Permutation (b_CN, development): **p = 0.5697**.

Against the section 3 rule: development sign positive but permutation FAILS
(p=0.57 vs required <0.05); holdout pooled +0.495 is under the +1.0 economic
bar; and the two holdout seasons carry OPPOSITE signs (-1.9 then +2.9) — the
signature of noise, not a bias.

**Interpretation.** The lookahead audit's +0.95 (t=2.81) collapses to +0.29
(p=0.57) the moment the labels become bettor-feasible. The descriptive bias
was therefore substantially a LABEL-SELECTION ARTIFACT — conditioning team
class on full-season outcomes selects on components of the very errors being
measured — and not a market mispricing. Section 0 anticipated exactly this
discount. Per section 3, the team-class question is CLOSED: no re-thresholding,
no alternative class definitions, no subsetting.

The audit's other finding stands unchanged: market PRECISION (MAE/RMSE) is
identical across classes — the book prices tanking-team games as accurately
as any others.
