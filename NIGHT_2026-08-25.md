## Confirmed defect, to fix before the next slate

The model arms on `(halftime or period >= 3)` while E is computed from H1
only, so it stays armed all game on a frozen number. On CHI@CONN it produced a
22c phantom "edge" in Q3 while the market had correctly absorbed 10 points
scored in 72 seconds — a time-updated version of our own model sat within 0.6
pts of the market. 874 graded would-trade rows on that game, only 141 of them
at halftime; the rest are drift.

The grader of record is unaffected (`canonical_trade` takes the earliest armed
poll), but the dashboard misleads and the logs carry ~5x more would-trade rows
than are meaningful.

**Fix:** arm only inside the halftime break, and add the late-start guard so
`pre_L` refuses to freeze on a game already in progress.
